# Flip Tracker update log

## v1.1.0 · 2026-11-12
### Shared flip data
- Flip results you see in chat now go into one shared pool, and you get everyone else's. A player's last 6 and streak stay right even for games played while you were offline, so an old win streak no longer hides a losing run you missed.
- Your own flips that finished while you were away come back with the real opponent, item and time. They show on the Offline tab and in a "While you were away" toast.
- History-book flips get their real time, and "You won the itemflip!" results with no opponent get the name and item filled in.
- The same flip seen by several players is stored once. Times come from the server, so a wrong PC clock can't misplace anything.
- On first sync it loads the last 30 days and shares your last 30 days of history. Turn it off with Share flips on the Updates tab.
### Updates
- New Updates tab (press 0): your version, auto-update, shared data status and this update log.
- Auto-update from GitHub. New versions download in the background, are checked against a signature, and install when you close Minecraft.
- /flips update checks now, /flips sync syncs now.

## v1.0.0 · 2026-10-27
